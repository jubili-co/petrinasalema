# Tegelweg house papers — asset library

Reusable artwork extracted from scans of the original 1976 planning
submission for Tegelweg 4, Wien XXII (the storey addition, "Plan für die
Aufstockung des Hauses"). Hand-drafted linework, hand lettering, aged
paper, Austrian revenue stamps. These are the house's own biography in
documents, which is why they carry the site's premise so well. Direction,
registers, and reuse rules live in the [Illustration entry of
`IDEAS.md`](../../IDEAS.md).

Generated by [`scripts/tegelweg_papers.py`](../../scripts/tegelweg_papers.py).

## Redaction

The 1976 owners' names, signatures, home address, and the builder stamp
are removed from every asset: alpha-zeroed in line extractions, healed
with the estimated paper background in color crops, or cropped away
entirely. The project's own address (Tegelweg 4, Leopoldau) stays. The
source PDFs are deliberately not committed because they contain the
unredacted names; keep them in private storage. After any regeneration,
re-verify the redaction zones before publishing.

## Folders

- `full/` — 12 complete sheets. `*-line.webp` is transparent brown line
  art (lossless), `*-line-blue.webp` is the same alpha in Dotto blue
  (`#45519f`). `*-cream.webp` is flattened on the site cream. Four
  sheets with original color tints also ship as `*-tinted.webp`
  (transparent, hues preserved: the pink/green/yellow structural tints).
- `details/` — 22 vignettes (plans without margins, the house portraits,
  the hand-written area tables, title block, stair / bath / balcony /
  terrace cuts). Line, line-blue, and cream variants.
- `words/` — 15 hand-lettered words and phrases (STRASSENANSICHT,
  HOFANSICHT, ERDGESCHOSS, MANSARDE, "x) Dachfenster", ZENTRAL BEHEIZT…).
  Useful as headings and chrome. Line, line-blue, and cream variants.
- `objects/` — 4 original-color document crops (revenue stamps, area
  table on aged paper, healed title block). Use as photographed objects,
  not as extracted line art.
- `social/` — 8 composed, post-ready tiles: 1080×1080, 1080×1350 (4:5),
  1080×1920 (9:16).

## Naming

`{sheet}-{variant}.webp` for full sheets, `{sheet}--{detail}-{variant}`
for details. Sheet keys: `neu-erdgeschoss`, `neu-obergeschoss`,
`neu-mansarde`, `neu-keller` (the 1976 "new" state), `alt-erdgeschoss`,
`alt-obergeschoss`, `alt-keller` (the pre-1976 state), `schnitt-a-b`,
`schnitt-c-d` (sections), `strassenansicht`, `hofansicht` (elevations),
`bereiche` (the stamped area-summary sheet).

## Usage rules

- Line color is baked in: Dotto brown (`#633b2f`) on `*-line.webp`,
  Dotto blue (`#45519f`) on `*-line-blue.webp`. The site decorative
  register uses the blue set at `/papers/blue/`; brown originals stay
  at `/papers/`. Transparent line assets sit directly on
  `--canvas` surfaces; that is the intended pairing.
- Ghost register (background × chrome): use the transparent `-line`
  assets at CSS `opacity: 0.12–0.18`. Do not pre-bake ghost variants.
- Honest provenance: full strength anywhere on Tegelweg pages; ghosted or
  cropped on non-project surfaces (home, studio, book, 404, OG, posts)
  where no project claim is implied; never under another project's
  photography.
- The Alt/Neu pair documents the 1976 addition by the previous owners,
  not Petrina's redesign. Caption as the house's papers, not as studio
  work.
- Scans are ~200 dpi. They hold up to roughly 1000 px display width;
  avoid extreme zoom crops beyond what `details/` already provides.

## Regeneration

```sh
pip install pymupdf pillow numpy
UPLOADS_DIR=/path/to/source-pdfs python3 scripts/tegelweg_papers.py

# Recolor committed line assets to blue without the source PDFs:
python3 scripts/tegelweg_papers.py --from-existing
```
