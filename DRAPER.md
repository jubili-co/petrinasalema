## One-line mandate

> Stop being a portfolio of a designer. Become the place where someone realizes their apartment has been writing their biography, and decides to rewrite it.

## How to say the idea (locked temperature)

This is the speaking version of the mandate. It is the temperature we want on the site: full sentences, glad to explain, simple. Not a slogan stack. Not telegram.

A good homepage does not start by listing services. It starts with a feeling the visitor already has, then offers a first step.

Your rooms are already shaping how you live, even if you never chose that. Most people decorate around that. This practice is for the moment someone notices, and decides to change the room instead of white-knuckling a better life inside a bad one.

If that lands, the visitor should not think “I hired a decorator.” They should think “I am someone whose life is designed on purpose.”

The first button should feel like stepping into that, not like booking a haircut. A free 30-minute call can still feel serious. The label on the button is what makes the difference.

## The wound verb (open)

The wound needs a verb with agency: the room is doing something to you. *Shaping* is Petrina’s natural word, and it is a little too kind for the problem. *Deciding* has teeth, and it does not sound like her.

Keep *shape* for the work she does (rooms that shape how you want to live). Use a stronger, spoken verb for the problem.

Spoken options with similar force to *deciding*:

- **make** — “Your rooms already make you live a certain way.” Plain. Easy to say. Preferred draft in [`COPY.md`](./COPY.md).
- **write** — “Your rooms are already writing how you live.” Closest to the mandate (the apartment writing a biography). A little more literary.
- **run** — “Your rooms are already running your days.” Concrete. About time, not taste.

Do not go back to *defining* (sociology) or *influencing* (no threat). Do not force *deciding* if it fails the aloud test in her mouth.
