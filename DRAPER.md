# Draper

Marketing and positioning logic for this site. Modeled on how Don Draper actually works in the pitches and fights that define him: not “make it sexy,” but a few recurring moves.

Use this when writing or judging visitor-facing copy, IA, offers, and brand extensions (Studio, Book, Work, About, Materiality, Jubili). Pair with the tone rules in `AGENTS.md` (human register). Draper is the *idea*. Tone is the *voice*.

## One-line mandate

> Stop being a portfolio of a designer. Become the place where someone realizes their apartment has been writing their biography, and decides to rewrite it.

Everything else (Cal links, stacked photos, tone of voice, legal dodge) is production design for that idea.

## The nine moves

### 1. Start from a human wound, not a product

Kodak isn’t a projector. It’s a machine that lets you revisit the life you already lived and can’t get back. The product is the alibi. The sale is grief, nostalgia, private religion.

**On this site:** Don’t lead with layout, FF&E, or “interiors.” Lead with the fact that rooms already decide how people live, and most people never notice.

### 2. When the truth is blocked, invent a sharper allowed truth

Lucky Strike can’t say “healthy.” So: “It’s toasted.” Constraint isn’t a problem. It’s the brief.

**On this site:** The practice cannot sell “architecture” or “interior design” as licensed services. That wall is the brief. Sell the human verb: shape life, rest / gather / think, fix the rooms. Biography may state training. Commercial pages sell the outcome, not the licensed noun.

### 3. One idea. Kill the rest

He doesn’t stack benefits. He finds the sentence that reorders the room, then everything else serves that sentence. Research, features, and side offers are noise until the idea is locked.

**On this site:** Prefer one emotional door. Fit call and Jumpstart can both exist as commerce, but they should feel like two keys to the same kingdom, not two religions. Brand extensions (Materiality, Jubili) only earn space if they express the same idea downstream.

### 4. Aspiration is identity theft with consent

People don’t buy the object. They buy a temporary seat in a better story about themselves.

**On this site:** The close is not “I hired a decorator.” It is “I am someone whose life is designed on purpose.”

### 5. Desire runs on dissatisfaction with the present self

Advertising sells happiness, but this version of happiness is relief from emptiness: status, romance, control, belonging, a life that looks intentional. Use that cleanly. Don’t moralize it away into soft lifestyle copy.

**On this site:** “Nicer room” is the false ask. The real ask is life toward how they want to live.

### 6. Show, don’t argue

The carousel pitch works because the room *feels* something before it agrees. Images and silence do half the close. Copy that explains too much is amateur.

**On this site:** Work imagery carries proof. Copy names the wound and the offer. Don’t lecture with ethos posters when a room already made the point. Titles like “Use over spectacle” are doctrine, not observation.

### 7. Scarcity is moral theater

Few clients, high price, closed door. Not only economics. It tells the buyer: you were chosen into seriousness. Exclusion is part of the product.

**On this site:** “A few projects a year” and paid Jumpstart are initiation signals, not only capacity notes. Don’t dilute them with endless soft CTAs.

### 8. Change the conversation if you’re losing

If the category is “decorators and mood boards,” don’t win inside it. Rename the game.

**On this site:** Refuse the mood-board category. The game is authorship of daily life (and guest experience), not styling.

### 9. Reinvention is the deepest product

Dick Whitman becomes Don Draper. Brands that work let people do a smaller version of that: step out of an old life into a named new one.

**On this site:** A project is a chapter break. The rooms are how the new chapter gets lived.

## Core mechanism (this practice)

Under the elite line of the site, one mechanism:

**Your rooms are already parenting you. Most people never notice. This practice sells the moment you take the parenting back.**

Recognition → authorship → permission through scarcity → borrowed identity.

Recognition, in one line: **Stop decorating around a life you didn't choose.**

**Borrowed identity:** not “I hired a decorator,” but “I am someone whose life is designed on purpose.”

**CTAs are initiation, not checkout.** A fit call or Jumpstart is not a calendar errand or a price comparison. It is a moment of taking authorship over the rooms that will shape habits, and over time who they become. Labels and microcopy should feel like stepping into seriousness. Reject pure logistics (“Book now”, “Schedule a call”) and pure economics (price alone with no meaning). Price and time may stay as facts under the button. The button itself should carry the chapter-break.

**Authorship is not willpower.** It is not “I will decide to rest, gather, and think better.” People fail that every January. Authorship here means shaping the cocoon: the room that holds them, so life can lean toward how they want to live. Change the container. The days follow.

**Causal chain:** spaces shape habits → habits shape what people become.

Same mechanism as the cocoon, said in behavioral language. Habits are often downstream of layout, friction, light, where the chair is, what the room invites. You don’t white-knuckle a better evening into a bad room. You change the room so the better habit is the path of least resistance. Over time, those habits are what someone becomes.

The practice intervenes at the first link (the space). It does not sell grit, morning routines, or personality change. Those are consequence. Habits and becoming may be named as what follows a room. Never as the product.

Do not write copy that sounds like habit coaching or self-help grit (“build better habits”). Write copy about fixing and shaping rooms.

**Same mechanism, different mask:**

- **Home:** the cocoon shapes how they live and who they become.
- **Hospitality:** the guest should feel the life the owner wishes to project.
- **Commercial:** the space trains behavior toward an outcome (a P&L in built form).

**Jubili and Materiality only become elite if they are downstream expressions of the same theft-of-authorship idea:**

- **Jubili:** yield-designed rooms (hospitality mask of the mechanism).
- **Materiality:** kits that let someone act without full initiation (a smaller key to the same kingdom).

If they read as “also I sell other things,” the conversation snaps back to retail. Don would call that losing.

**Hard filter for extensions (Studio Notes, Materiality, Jubili, About shelves):**

> Is this one woman’s authority, or a holding company apologizing for having side hustles?

If the page or chrome answers “holding company,” cut or demote until the name carries one gravity again. Downstream is allowed. Parallel brands in the primary path are not.

## How the pitch is built

- Open with an observation about human nature (the wound). Land the offer as the inevitable answer. Do not open with services, credentials, or a feature stack.
- One idea reorders the room. Then proof (Work), initiation (Book / Cal), and scarcity. Kill competing ideas.
- Certainty over hedge. Prefer a clear sentence to a balanced paragraph that protects every edge case.
- Instinct over research-as-safety. Surveys and diagnostics may exist as optional recognition doors (see `IDEAS.md`). They must never replace the idea or gate the calendar as bureaucracy.
- Class and belonging: scarcity and price are not only economics. They seat the buyer at the adult table. Cheapening the initiation (retail BNPL badges on Fit call, endless soft CTAs) weakens the seat.

## Word choice that matters

- **Deciding** beats **defining.** “Your space is already deciding how you live” has agency and threat. “Defining” is sociology. Prefer the wound that feels alive.
- **Shape the cocoon / the room** beats **decide to live better.** Willpower copy is self-help. Container copy is this practice.
- Reject clever oppositions as lead ideas: `considered rather than styled`, `use over spectacle`, `X rather than Y` as aesthetic slogan. That is category language (mood-board war). He wants the wound, not a taste badge.
- Home SEO, About blurbs, and Studio principles are common places this sludge returns. Audit them when the hero has already moved on.

## Site audit: protect

- **Book’s Don sentence** (and its kin): space already deciding how you live; stop decorating around a life you didn’t choose; shape the room that holds the life you want.
- **The home echo pair.** Hook: “Your rooms are already deciding how you live.” Answer: “I help people change what the rooms decide.” The second line reuses the hook’s verb and flips the agency. Prefer this echo over remedial verbs (“fix”) at masthead level; “fix” stays right on practical pages (Studio, Book).
- **Studio lead** in the same family: nicer room is the false ask; the room should shape life toward how they want to live; value prop about fixing rooms that shape living and guest feeling.
- **Work as proof, not brochure.** Rooms as evidence. Imagery does half the close (move 6).
- **Legal constraint as Lucky Strike.** Cannot sell licensed “architecture / interior design” on commercial pages. Sell the human verb. Biography may state training.
- **Scarcity as initiation.** A few projects a year; paid Jumpstart; free fit call as the low-risk first irreversible-feeling step.

## Site audit: cut or distrust

- **Footer / chrome sprawl** (Materiality, Jubili, Studio Notes) without one empire idea. If the name shares gravity with a holding-company menu, demote until one woman’s authority is obvious.
- **About as multi-panel brand shelf** (Petrina / Jubili / Materiality as co-equal chapters). That is org-chart marketing. About may prove the person. It must not apologize for side hustles in the primary story.
- **Aesthetic SEO and leftover craft slogans** still humming after Book/Studio moved to the wound (e.g. “considered rather than styled” in `home.json`).
- **Two CTAs as two religions.** Fit call + Jumpstart is smart commerce only if both keys open the same kingdom. One emotional door; two practical sizes.
- **Ethos-poster principles.** Teacherly doctrine titles. Prefer observation (“If it only looks good in photos…”) or cut the block.
- **Explaining too much.** If Work already made the point, copy should not lecture.

## Production design

Cal links, stacked photos, type, tone of voice, and the legal dodge are **production design for the idea**. They are not the idea. When polishing UI or copy, ask whether the change serves the one-line mandate or only makes a prettier portfolio of a designer.
