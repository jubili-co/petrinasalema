## Book page — funnel pull formula

1. Name the hidden problem (hook)
2. Mirror the right identity (fit)
3. Offer a primary irreversible-feeling step that’s actually low risk (Cal)
4. Offer a valuable secondary for the not-ready (diagnostic / paid jumpstart)
5. Prove you’re selective and real (one sharp proof line)
6. Make email the side door, not a peer CTA

**One-line standard:** If a stranger can’t feel pulled in 8 seconds — before understanding your services — it’s still a brochure. Elite Book pages sell recognition first, logistics second.

## Locked — Room Scheme price architecture (2026-08)

Decision for shoppable moodboard packs. Do not revisit until a kill criterion fires.

### Choice

**Primary product: premium flat Room Scheme at €390.**

Not commodity PDF. Not % of FF&E as the billing model.

### Why this wins

| Model | Verdict | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commodity PDF (~€25–65) | Reject as primary | Race with Etsy volume sellers. Needs distribution Petrina does not have. Trains buyers to undervalue the work. Weakens hospitality / hireability signal on the site. |
| % of FF&E (10–15%) | Reject as pack billing | Correct for full-service procurement. Awkward for a self-serve digital pack where the buyer shops retail links. Unclear checkout, hard Stripe MVP, feels like a quote not a product. |
| Premium flat scheme (€390) | **Lock** | Matches real comps (Effortless ~£350 schemes, Mondän ~$497 room packages). Fits time-poor, not budget-poor buyer. One Stripe price. Protects brand next to Jubili. Viable at low volume. |

**Use 10–15% only as value copy**, not as the price formula. Example: “Design fee is about 12% of a typical €3,200 room spend.” The charge stays a flat €390.

### What ships in a Room Scheme

From a **real completed project**, not a generic moodboard:

1. Room moodboard / scheme visual
2. Full ready-to-order shopping list with live links
3. Indicative room budget band (furniture + key soft goods)
4. Short install / order notes (what to buy first, what can wait)
5. Stated fit: room type + rough size band (honest about non-custom)

Maintenance rule: link rot is ops debt. Budget time to refresh links, or state a substitution email path.

### Offer ladder (keep distinct)

These are different jobs. Do not price them as the same product at three sizes.

| Offer | Price | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Room Scheme | **€390** | Steal a finished room. Async. No call. |
| Design Jumpstart | **€290** (live) | One hour on *their* space. DIY plan for the highest-leverage change. |
| Fit call → full project | Custom | Selective residential / remote projects. |
| Jubili hospitality | Separate lane | STR → mid-term, small hospitality. Not on the consumer scheme grid. |

Optional later add-on (only after first schemes sell): **Fit to my room +€250** (layout tweak + swap list). Bridges scheme → Jumpstart without inventing a fourth core product.

### Channel implications

- Site: schemes live under studio as a secondary door, not the hero. Jubili / projects stay primary for hireability.
- Discovery: Pinterest + project Reels first. Etsy optional for 1–2 schemes as a side door, same €390 price (do not discount into commodity).
- Ads: no Meta scale test until one organic or warm sale. A €100–200 smoke test is allowed after creative exists; it is not the launch plan.

### MVP test (cheap)

1. Pick 3 real projects → 3 schemes.
2. One simple page + Stripe Payment Link at €390.
3. Deliver as PDF + shopping list (Notion/Airtable/CSV fine).
4. Run for a fixed window with Pinterest/IG only.

### Kill / keep criteria

**Keep and expand** if, within the test window: ≥2 paid schemes, or 1 paid scheme + clear repeat intent / waitlist, without heavy custom support per sale.

**Kill or reprice** if: zero sales after real distribution effort, or every buyer demands custom layout support (then the product is actually Jumpstart, not a scheme).

**Do not** respond to slow sales by dropping to €49. That switches architecture to commodity. Either improve proof/distribution or stop.
