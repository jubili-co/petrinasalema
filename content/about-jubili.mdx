Jubili is not a sister company sitting next to this practice. It is the

numbers that have to hold. Ideas were tested there before they were offered
to clients.

Petrina built the brand and led design, styling, and guest experience for its
first rental in Vienna. It has been running for five years. The property
reached Superhost within its first six months, which it has held since, and
carries Guest Favorite recognition.

The brief was simple. Delight the guest who pays to stay, and keep the numbers
working for the owner who owns the home.

**2 → 4 bedrooms** (4 → 8 guests), redesigned while the home stayed in operation.
