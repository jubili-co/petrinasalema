Jubili began as this practice's ideas, tested on paying guests. Petrina
co-founded the brand and led design, styling, and guest experience for its
first rental, a live test bed for five years. The property reached Superhost
within its first six months, has held it since, and carries Guest Favorite
recognition.

The brief was simple. Delight the guest who pays to stay, and keep the numbers
working for the owner whose home it is.

## In numbers

**4.97/5** average rating across 120+ guest reviews.

**€120k+** in booking revenue.

**2 → 4 bedrooms** (4 → 8 guests), redesigned while the home stayed in operation.

One property, many cycles. The choices that hold guest scores also cut turnover
and make the home steadier to earn from.
