Petrina has a background in architecture and interior design, and is based in
Vienna. She trained with an MSc in Architecture from [Chalmers University of
Technology (Sweden)](https://www.chalmers.se/en/) and a BA (with honors) in
Interior Design from [Bournemouth University (United
Kingdom)](https://www.bournemouth.ac.uk/). Her work draws on more than a decade
across Tanzania, Sweden, the United Kingdom, and Austria, on projects in
healthcare, civic and institutional buildings, and residential and hospitality
interiors.

She founded her independent practice in 2021 on a simple observation. Most
rooms were never asked to support the life people want in them. The practice
takes on a few projects a year, transforming a space through planning, material
sourcing and styling. Projects are done in-person in Vienna, and remotely
abroad.

This portfolio gathers a full range of projects from her years inside
architecture practices, and the independent work that followed, including the
homes that became [Jubili](https://jubili.co), the hospitality brand co-founded
by her practice.
