## ABOUT PETRINA

I'm trained as an architect and interior designer, with an MSc in Architecture
from Chalmers University of Technology (Sweden) and a BA (Hons) in Interior
Design from Bournemouth University (United Kingdom). Over more than a decade,
I've worked across Tanzania, Sweden, the United Kingdom, and Austria, on
projects spanning healthcare, civic and institutional buildings, and residential
and hospitality interiors.

In 2021, I founded my own independent design practice in Vienna. This portfolio
brings together that full range: work from my time inside architecture
practices, and the independent projects that followed, including the properties
that became the foundation of Jubili, a hospitality brand I later co-founded.
