Petrina is an architect and interior designer based in Vienna. Trained with an
MSc in Architecture from [Chalmers University of Technology
(Sweden)](https://www.chalmers.se/en/) and a BA (With honors) in Interior Design from
[Bournemouth University (United Kingdom)](https://www.bournemouth.ac.uk/), her
work is shaped by more than a decade across Tanzania, Sweden, the United
Kingdom, and Austria, on healthcare, civic and institutional buildings, and
residential and hospitality interiors.

She founded her independent practice in 2021. The work tends toward spaces that
feel considered rather than styled: clear in plan, grounded in material, and
built to hold up in use. She takes on a focused set of projects each year, from
space planning through interiors, with clients in Vienna, abroad, and remotely.

This portfolio gathers that full range: projects from her years inside
architecture practices, and the independent work that followed, including the
properties that became the foundation of [Jubili](https://jubili.co), a
hospitality design brand she later co-founded.
