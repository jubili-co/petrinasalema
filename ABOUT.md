# Petrina Salema

**Headline:** Design Principal for Spaces & Interiors

**Sub-line:** Trained in architecture and interiors. Work across residential, hospitality, healthcare, and civic spaces, from Tanzania to Austria.

## About

I'm trained as an architect and interior designer, with an MSc in Architecture from Chalmers University of Technology (Sweden) and a BA (Hons) in Interior Design from Bournemouth University (United Kingdom). Over more than a decade, I've worked across Tanzania, Sweden, the United Kingdom, and Austria, on projects spanning healthcare, civic and institutional buildings, and residential and hospitality interiors.

In 2021, I founded my own independent design practice in Vienna. Work happens in Vienna, abroad, and remotely. This portfolio brings together that full range: work from my time inside architecture practices, and the independent projects that followed, including the properties that became the foundation of Jubili, a hospitality design brand I later co-founded.

## Featured work

### Tegelweg — 4-Bedroom Apartment, Vienna

Residential interior design / hospitality conversion · Design lead, Petrina Salema · Full interior concept, space planning, FF&E sourcing and styling

Designed and grown in phases over five years, from an initial 2-bedroom configuration to its current 4 bedrooms, this project is where interior concept, space planning, and guest-experience design were tested and refined against real, ongoing use. Designed for Jubili, a hospitality design brand co-founded as part of establishing this practice.

### Brabbeegasse — Single-Unit Apartment, Vienna

Residential interior design / hospitality conversion · Design lead, Petrina Salema · Concept, styling, sourcing

Designed for Jubili as the brand expanded to other properties. This project demonstrated that a rented space can be transformed to reflect a brand's aesthetic and also be returned to its original state once the leasing period ends: an important proof of concept for the reversibility of smart design interventions.
